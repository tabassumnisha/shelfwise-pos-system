Everything a small shop needs at the counter and in the back room

  • Fast terminal

    Tiles, search and barcode entry. Discount, cash change, card and mobile payments, and a printable receipt.

  • Server-side pricing

    The browser sends product ids and quantities only. Prices, tax, discount limits and stock are decided in one database transaction.

  • Live stock

    Every sale, refund, delivery and manual change is logged. Stock cannot go below zero.

  • Suppliers and orders

    Low-stock alerts, one-click reorder, purchase orders and receiving that updates stock and cost.

  • Refunds

    Admins refund a sale and the items return to stock, with the action recorded.

  • Reports

    Revenue per day, profit, average sale, top sellers and payment mix for 7, 14 or 30 days.

  • Roles

    Cashiers sell and see their own sales. Admins manage products, stock, team and reports. Every route checks the role on the server.

  • Ready to deploy

    Docker, Render and Vercel demo files, SQL schema and seed scripts, tests and full documentation.
